Privacy Policy

2i ("the Company") values the personal data of data subjects and complies with applicable laws.

This is a courtesy translation. The Korean version prevails.

1. Personal Data We Collect

The Company collects the following items for consultation inquiries.

  • Required: name, company name, industry, contact information (email or phone)
  • Optional: inquiry details (description of your situation)

This website is a static site and does not use any automated collection tools (cookie-based tracking or behavioral analytics). Consultation requests are sent directly to the Company's contact address through the user's own email client.

2. Purpose of Collection and Use

Personal data collected is used only to respond to consultation requests, provide service information, and contact you regarding your inquiry.

3. Retention and Use Period

Personal data is destroyed without delay once the consultation or inquiry has been handled. Where retention is required under applicable law, it is kept for that period. If a data subject requests deletion, it is destroyed immediately.

4. Third-Party Disclosure and Outsourced Processing

The Company does not disclose personal data to any external party. This website is hosted through GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), and consultation content is transmitted using the user's own email service.

5. Rights of Data Subjects

Data subjects may at any time request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of their personal data. Requests may be directed to the Data Protection Officer listed below, and the Company will act on them without delay.

6. Data Protection Officer

  • Officer: Geumji Han (Principal)
  • Contact: contact@2icorp.site

7. Destruction of Personal Data

Personal data for which the purpose of collection and use has been fulfilled is destroyed without delay, and electronic files are deleted using methods that prevent recovery.

8. Notification Obligation

Any changes to this policy will be announced on this website.
