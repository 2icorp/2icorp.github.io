The 2i gauge method · Services

We don't guess. Measure → verify → hand off.

You only spend on the next stage after clearing a gate. It's a low-risk sequence: start small, and scale only what works. This structure removes the biggest fear head-on: "what if we spend the money and it doesn't work?"

  1. 01
    Measure gateMEASURE · Diagnosis

    We pin the problem down in numbers. We measure the baseline of your floor-level KPIs and the state of your data, so we know exactly what to improve and by how much.

  2. 02
    Verify gateVERIFY · PoC

    We pick one point and actually measure the before and after. If it doesn't hit the target delta, we don't scale it. Real money only comes after proof.

  3. 03
    Hand-off gateOPERATE · Build

    We scale only what's been verified and hand it to your operations team to run on its own. We stay with you, documentation and training included, until it's delivering results.

The robotics & automation era

The money flows to automated factories. The bottleneck is readiness.

AI is moving past chatbots into Physical AI, attaching to sensors, equipment, and robots, and that's where capital and government budgets are converging. But bring in a robotic arm with no data, vision, signal, or judgment to drive it, and you've bought an expensive decoration. Most mid-size manufacturers aren't ready yet, which is exactly why now is the entry point. 2i builds the software brain layer that turns automation into actual returns.

What automation needs Eyes · seeing and judging 2i · gives robots and inspection lines judgment with vision inspection and defect-detection AI
What automation needs Senses · reading state through signal 2i · uses signal fingerprinting (sigprint) to catch wireless interference and equipment anomalies in real time, keeping the line running
What automation needs Data · information that flows 2i · consolidates scattered data into something predictable, fueling automated decision-making
What automation needs Brain · handling things on its own 2i · uses custom AI agents to handle orders, inquiries, and document flow with no human hands

And a substantial share of that transformation cost can be covered by government AX vouchers and automation support programs. 2i designs the technology and the funding application together, helping you become one of the companies that's ready.
