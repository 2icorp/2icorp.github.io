What we do

Problems at small and mid-market companies are small and non-standard, so large SI firms rarely take them on. 2i exists to fill exactly that gap. We measure the real bottleneck on the floor in numbers, let the cheapest method go first, and bring in expensive technology only when it is truly needed.

How we work · the gates

Every project passes through three gates. Measure (a two to three week diagnostic that puts a number on the problem), Prove (confirm the effect with real floor data), and Transfer (your own team takes over operation). Budget for the next stage is only spent once each gate is cleared. This structure removes the biggest fear in AI adoption: what if we spend the money and it does not work?

What we offer

  • AX diagnostic sprints, PoC validation, build and transfer
  • Floor automation and data integration for manufacturing, distribution, and logistics
  • AI training and workshops
  • Design linked to government AX vouchers and automation support programs

Augmenting, not replacing

What 2i builds is not a system that removes people, but a tool that helps people focus on what matters. We automate the repetition and leave the judgment to you.
